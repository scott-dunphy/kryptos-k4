# Kryptos compass / lodestone research (web, 2026-10-17)

## Bottom line
No source found gives a measured needle bearing. Reports conflict; confidence in any specific bearing is LOW.

## 1. Descriptions
- CIA virtual tour: "a lodestone ... co-located with a navigational compass rose." https://www.cia.gov/legacy/headquarters/kryptos-sculpture/
- Wikipedia: "one of the stone slabs has an engraving of a compass rose pointing to a lodestone." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kryptos
- Elonka Dunin aerial page: compass on south side of walkway, green area near entrance (exact spot uncertain); "needle ... appears to be pointing towards another small stone nearby, which is probably a magnetized rock, or lodestone." https://elonka.com/kryptos/KryptosAerial.html
- Elonka FAQ: "engraved compass with a needle pointing at a lodestone"; no bearing. https://www.elonka.com/kryptos/faq.html
- Sanborn letter 15 Dec 1989 (quoted on scirealm): "At the entrance a lodestone ... refers to ancient navigational compasses." https://scirealm.org/KryptosHints.html
- Quoted newspaper text (WSJ 27 May 2005 per scirealm; same text reprinted elsewhere): "a compass needle carved onto one of the rocks is pulled off due north by a lodestone that Mr. Sanborn placed nearby." Note: this says the lodestone pulls the needle to due north, i.e. the lodestone is (magnetically) north of the needle's pivot, but the carved needle itself need not point north. (I could not verify the 1990 attribution; one search summary called it a 2005 reprint.)

## 2. Bearing: conflicting reports (all unverified)
- Elonka aerial page: "One report is that the 'north' direction on the compass rose is pointing roughly south-southwest (around 220 degrees) but this is not exact." (reading: rose rotated so N is ~220 deg true)
- scirealm.org Details: "south point of the needle pointing approximately 15-30 degrees North of East" (i.e. bearing ~60-75 deg if measured from east toward north... = roughly ENE-ish; 67.5 is within/near this), claimed roughly the great-circle direction toward Valley of the Kings, Egypt (speculative). https://scirealm.org/KryptosDetails.html
- Numberworld blog (2020): "i dont really know if the needle points to ENE or the WSW, it depends on which color to you define for North"; asserts ENE is "exactly the direction illustrated on the compass rose." Comments there: "ENE is 67 1/2 degrees"; one commenter suggests Berlin Wall/Rotes Rathaus (ENE of it) link. https://www.numberworld.blog/2020/07/kryptos-cipher-part-4.html
- A (solvingkryptos.com-attributed) summary claiming "WSW" and a true E/W etched stone could not be fetched (domain dead); treat as unverified.
- Note: ENE (67.5 deg) and WSW (247.5 deg) are opposite ends of one line; 220 deg (SSW report) is ~27 deg off WSW. Needle line ENE/WSW is plausible across reports, but nobody published a survey/compass measurement.
- Lodestone is described only as "nearby" the needle; no direction given.

## 3. Geography / bearing computation (spherical formula, my calculation)
Initial great-circle bearing from CIA HQ (38.952N, 77.146W):
- Berlin Alexanderplatz (52.522N, 13.413E): 44.4 deg (NE)
- Weltzeituhr (52.5209N, 13.4135E): 44.4 deg
- Rotes Rathaus: 44.4 deg
- Reverse bearing Berlin -> Langley: 296.5 deg
So great-circle direction to Berlin (~44 deg) is ~23 deg from ENE (67.5). A rhumb line / flat-map (Mercator) bearing to Berlin would be ~ more easterly (~ 60-70 deg) - not computed here. Egypt claim (scirealm) not checked. I found no source on what lies along the needle line within the CIA grounds.

No statement about the compass tied to K4. Similar compasses in his Invisible Forces (Arlington) and Find the Lodestone (Bethesda).
