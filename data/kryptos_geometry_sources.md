# Kryptos physical geometry: sources for a sun/shadow model (web research 2026-10-18)

Confidence labels: **measured** (someone measured on site), **published** (stated by a source, method unknown),
**estimated-from-photo** (my own estimate from images listed here), **unknown**.
Existing notes checked first: `data/compass_research.md`, `data/sculpture_anomalies.md` (compass bearing is still unresolved; nothing new found).

## 4. Orientation and surroundings

| Item | Value | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site | NW corner of the New Headquarters Building courtyard, outside the cafeteria | published | CIA page; Wikipedia |
| Viewing direction | "the viewer is facing roughly northwest towards the sculpture", so the **cipher (front) face points roughly SE** | published (Elonka, on-site 2002-10-18) | https://www.elonka.com/kryptos/ |
| Front-face normal | ~135-145 deg (SE), derived from the NE-SW chord axis in aerial imagery | estimated-from-photo | Esri World Imagery z20 at ~38.95227 N, 77.14577 W (my pixel pick; satellite lean error of a few m) |
| Sculpture coordinates | ~38.95227, -77.14577. The task's 38.9521, -77.1466 lands on the NHB roof, ~70 m west | estimated-from-photo | Esri imagery |
| K2 coordinate point | ~55 m from the sculpture at bearing ~160 deg (agrees with Elonka's GPS "about 150' southeast") | estimated + measured (cheap GPS) | elonka.com/kryptos |
| Consistency check with sun | Gillogly's photos are dated 10/27/1999 (EXIF of digetal.jpg: 1999:10:27 14:23:16, Olympus C2000Z; clock accuracy unknown). At 14:23 EDT the sun is at az 207 deg, el 34 deg (my NOAA-style calc). Photos show the front lit from the left and letter-light falling on the paving behind (NW of) the cipher half. Elonka also had the afternoon sun "to our left". Both fit a SE-facing front | estimated | voynich.net photos; own calc |
| Blocking buildings | A low building wing ~8 m N; the NHB west wall/atrium ~13 m W (both distances from aerial, at 0.116 m/px); the cafeteria (white arched roof) lies to the E/SE across the lawn. Building heights unknown. The NHB windows behind the sculpture reflect sun back through the letters late in the afternoon (Elonka saw letters projected onto the white quartz rock by the glare) | estimated-from-photo (distances); published (glare) | Esri imagery; elonka.com/kryptos |
| Compass needle | Still unresolved: reports give ENE/WSW or "N toward ~220 deg" (see `data/compass_research.md`). The compass is at the entrance, south side of the walkway, not at the screen | published (conflicting) | elonka.com/kryptos/KryptosAerial.html ; scirealm.org/KryptosDetails.html |

## Gaps (unknown)
No published values for: curve radii, plan geometry, screen azimuth (only Elonka's "roughly northwest" view), bottom-row height, column/row pitch, whether rows are justified, pool depth or water level, surrounding building heights. The best path to real numbers is the LOC TIF plus Elonka's rubbings for scale. Site plans may be among the RR Auction archive items, but the lot listing names none.
