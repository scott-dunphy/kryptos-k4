# Sanborn's other encrypted works (source-reported only)

## Cyrillic Projector (UNC Charlotte; solved 2003)
Sources: https://scirealm.org/CyrillicProjector.html (author's own transcription/claims; most detail),
https://www.elonka.com/kryptos/faq.html, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrillic_Projector,
https://infocondb.org/con/def-con/def-con-12/kryptos-and-the-cracking-of-the-cyrillic-projector-cipher
Solvers: Frank Corr (reportedly early Jul 2003), Mike Bales (Sep 2003), Elonka Dunin's Kryptos Group led English translation/finish (Sep 2003).

1. Cipher: double-keyword Vigenere over Cyrillic (FAQ). One layer; NO transposition found in any source. Second face of sculpture is a Vigenere tableau (rows = keyed alphabet shifted by 1).
2. Keys (scirealm): KW1 = ТЕНЬ ("shadow") builds keyed alphabet ТЕНЬАБВГДЖЗИЙКЛМОПРСУФХШЦЧЩЪЫЭЮЯ (no Ё; the W-like letter (Ш) moved from normal position to sit next to Х -- "Alt-LU with ШЩ paired" noted as solution detail). KW2 = ЙБАСГТ (6 letters) selects tableau rows (Vigenere key); said to derive from clue word MEDUSA (meaning unknown). Page labels mode "N" (normal-alphabet lookup) vs Kryptos "K" (keyed). Kasiski/6-way frequency split reportedly used (secondary search summary, unverified).
3. Visual/spatial: sculpture is carved "inside-out" so light/shadow projects correctly on walls; 2" characters, bolts every 4"; clue word MEDUSA embedded mid-text in a different font on line 14 with an extra visible-nut bolt; lamp screws align with X/Y letters; from MEDUSA position wall shows "METAPHORS" from Adam Smith spinning top. These are scirealm author's observations/speculation, not solutions.
4. Errors: scirealm lists apparent typos in red: 21 (section 1), 4 (2a), 6 (2b); corrections credited to Michael Bales. Unknown whether intentional.
5. Plaintext: (1) Russian KGB-style text on psychologically controlling potential information sources; (2) 1982 KGB memo re Sakharov appeal / Pugwash Warsaw, signed Fedorchuk; continues on Antipodes.
6. K4 link: Elonka FAQ: Scheidt "not directly" involved; systems likely ones he taught Sanborn; shifted-alphabet tables closely resemble Kryptos. Wired 20 Jan 2005 (via https://www.kryptools.com.about.ag/hints.htm): Sanborn said the Projector has some of the same code as Kryptos "with some alterations", a deliberate "common denominator that I chose to leave in". Sanborn also said he designed visual encoding systems "much harder for cryptographers to crack", favors combining visual and machine-readable material. No source states the K4 method or that K4 uses the Projector keys.

NOT FOUND: no source mentions keyword letters left unchanged, half-alphabet/two-band behavior, or small numeric keys. Elonka's own Cyrillic pages/web.archive.org were not fetchable (403/blocked/404); the step-by-step solver write-ups were not retrieved.

## Antipodes (Hirshhorn-linked copper scrolls)
Source: https://elonka.com/kryptos/sanborn/antipodes.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipodes_(sculpture)
- Latin side repeats Kryptos ciphertext (order: part 3, part 4, space, parts 1-4, parts 1-2 truncated). Two extra dots correspond to "WW" in K2 plaintext ("Only WW"). Differences: spacing, dots, extra letters. Kryptos-only quirks: misaligned letters at start of K3, extra L at end of a tableau line.
- Cyrillic side = most Projector text plus extra section; fully solved by 23 Sep 2003. No separate method documented.

## Others
- Binary Systems (IRS Computing Ctr): binary/ASCII; other pieces use binary, many languages (Elonka FAQ). Cryptosystems he has used per FAQ: binary/ASCII, Vigenere, Caesar, transposition. No solved-method details found.
- Code Room, Covert Obsolescence, Lux, Radiance, NOAA, Paley: searches returned nothing on solved ciphers.
- Unsolved/unsolved-status works listed by FAQ (Ex Nexum, Circulating Capital, Libros, Caloosahatchee Manuscripts, etc.) need linguists; no solutions found.
